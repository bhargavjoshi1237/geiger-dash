Announcement images: shortlist
=============================

Painted landscapes that match the "/" showcase backdrops (lib/showcase-backgrounds.js): soft, muted, lots of open sky.
All are public domain (The Met Open Access, CC0). They're free to use, and credit is optional.

Original URLs are full resolution (often several MB). Before using one, scale it to about 1600px wide, for example:
  ffmpeg -i original.jpg -vf "scale=1600:-2" -q:v 3 out.jpg
then upload it to Studio > Announcements > Media, or to the public "pfp" bucket.

Find more: https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1.1/search?isPublicDomain=true&hasImages=true&limit=20&q=<artist or subject>
Object details: https://collectionapi.metmuseum.org/public/collection/v1/objects/<id>  (use "primaryImage", the original)
